Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/u-pangg7-everyday-wonder-two-days-to-find-the-line-970432d3) competition
"Everyday Wonder, Two Days to Find the Line": a program that writes one
sentence (at most 200 characters) about a small moment of wonder in
everyday life to `/output/entry.txt`.

Run it:

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The entry sentence (117 characters):

> The fridge light came on at midnight and my leftover rice glowed like a small city that had stayed up waiting for me.
